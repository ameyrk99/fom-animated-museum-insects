### My museum mods

- [Animated Museum Insects](https://www.nexusmods.com/fieldsofmistria/mods/1128) - Museum display insects use their real animation
- [Animated Museum Fish](https://www.nexusmods.com/fieldsofmistria/mods/1150) - Museum display fish swim around or flop (like Magikarp!)
